# FoxVale Seekers Terms of Use

Effective October 24, 2026.

FoxVale Seekers is a private utility operated by the FoxVale community through u/FoxValeSMP. Its purpose is to help authorized FoxVale staff find public Minecraft server requests and review whether FoxVale might meet those requests. It is not a public recruitment service or a player background-check tool.

## Permitted use

Staff may use an alert to open its source Reddit post, read the full request and community rules, and decide whether a relevant human response is welcome. Follow Reddit's rules, each community's recruitment and reply restrictions, and the person's stated contact preferences. An alert is a possible fit, not consent to contact, an endorsement, or confirmation that someone meets FoxVale's membership requirements.

Do not use this app for automated Reddit posts, comments, votes, or private messages; bulk outreach; harassment; sensitive profiling; account matching; selling data; advertising targeting; or AI training. Do not redistribute request excerpts, build dossiers, inspect posting histories through the app, or evade platform restrictions. Posts belonging to people explicitly under 18 are excluded from recruitment alerts.

## Operation and limitations

Checks are configured for approximately five-minute intervals. Timing, availability, completeness, matching accuracy, and delivery are not guaranteed. Keyword rules can miss suitable requests or include unsuitable ones. Staff must verify the request, age eligibility, Java access, version compatibility, and gameplay preferences before offering an invitation.

The app only reads Reddit content; it does not choose members or send Reddit replies. Alerts go to the configured private staff Discord channel and mention the configured Admin role. Only the operator may configure the integration. Protect the webhook credential and keep channel access limited to authorized staff.

## Data and removal

The [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) describes processing, recipients, retention, and deletion requests. Staff must honor corrections and deletion requests. Webhook alerts are removed when a source change is detected or after seven days, subject to service availability and prompt operator handling of failures. Before the operator stops the service permanently, remaining app-created copies must be removed. Access to new alerts may be suspended if cleanup or platform access fails.

## Availability and changes

The app is provided without a service-level commitment. The operator may change or discontinue it, including if required by Reddit or Discord. Material changes to its purpose or data practices require updating these documents. Reddit's applicable terms and policies continue to govern use of its services and content. These app terms do not grant rights to reuse someone else's content beyond those permissions.

For support, corrections, or deletion requests, contact [u/FoxValeSMP](https://www.reddit.com/user/FoxValeSMP/). FoxVale Seekers is not affiliated with or endorsed by Reddit, Discord, Microsoft, or Mojang.
